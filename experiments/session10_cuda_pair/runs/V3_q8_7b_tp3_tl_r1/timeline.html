Time per decode step, by rank

Timeline

Each rank has two rows. The compute row is device time, closed by a device sync. The communication row shows sends, waits and copies between the device and the host. A color is the other rank of that send or wait. All times are on the coordinator clock.

Bytes per link

Bytes on the wire, frame headers included, counted by the sender. Decode steps only, then the whole run with prefill.

Clock alignment

Offset of each rank's monotonic clock to the coordinator, from 16 round trips at the start and at the end. The error is at most half the round trip.
